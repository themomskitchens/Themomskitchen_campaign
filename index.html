🏆 Corporate Lucky Draw

📜 Official Campaign Terms & Conditions

  • Each verified registration will receive 1 Base Entry into the Corporate Lucky Draw.
  • For every ₹100 of confirmed billing, participants earn 1 Additional Entry. (Entries are calculated automatically.)
  • Upon completion of every 5 successful orders, the participant will receive 5 Bonus Entries as a performance reward.
  • The Winner will be declared based on the highest total confirmed orders during the campaign period.
  • In case of a tie, the participant with the higher total billing amount will be ranked higher.
  • Only corporate complex employees are eligible to participate.
  • Any misuse, duplicate registrations, or suspicious activity may lead to disqualification.
  • The final decision rests solely with Food Corner Management and will be considered final and binding.
📅 Campaign valid for limited period only. Entries close at month-end.
